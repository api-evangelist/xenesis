# Xenesis

> Xenesis, Inc. is an optical satellite communications company founded in 2017 by Mark LaPenna and headquartered at 3333 Warrenville Rd, Suite 200, Lisle, Illinois. It builds free-space optical (laser) communications terminals for space-to-ground and satellite-to-satellite data transport, and is developing a planned optical mesh network to backhaul high-bandwidth traffic.

Xenesis's core product is the Xen-Hub, a free-space optical communications terminal rated at greater than 10 Gbps and enabled by a technology transfer from NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory. The company positions optical links as faster, higher-capacity and harder to intercept than radio-frequency links or terrestrial fiber, and markets a phased network build — Intercessor — of satellites and ground stations intended to reach global coverage. Publicly reported milestones include a Space Development Agency optical-terminal award in August 2022 and a Phase 2 follow-on that kicked off in December 2023, built to the SDA OCT v3.1 and v4.0 standards; a payload agreement with Airbus for a demonstration slot on the Bartolomeo platform aboard the International Space Station; a $1.2M agreement with Georgia Tech for satellite optical communications; and a testing pact with MBS Lab.
